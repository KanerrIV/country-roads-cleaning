Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Country Roads Cleaning Solutions, LLC ("Country Roads," "we," "us") is a cleaning company based in Kenova, WV. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, request a quote, apply for a job, or respond to our ads (including Facebook and Instagram lead forms), and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, email address, and city or ZIP code.
  • Cleaning request details such as home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, type of cleaning, preferred schedule, and any notes you share.
  • Job application details such as availability, work experience, and answers you provide on our application.
  • Website usage data collected automatically, such as pages visited, device and browser type, and how you arrived at our site.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote request, schedule cleanings, and provide our services.
  • To review job applications and contact applicants.
  • To answer questions and provide customer support.
  • To understand how people find and use our website and to improve our advertising.

By submitting your information, you agree that we may contact you by phone call, text message, or email about your request. You can ask us to stop at any time.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Google (email, forms, spreadsheets, and Google Analytics), Meta (Facebook and Instagram lead ads), and Whop (website analytics), or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar tools from Google Analytics and Whop to measure visits and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, keep business records, or meet legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by calling or texting (304) 710-5430 or emailing countryroadscleaningsolutions@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Your quote request and privacy

When you request a quote, you share your name, ZIP code, home details, cleaning preferences, contact details, and any note you choose to include. Country Roads Cleaning Solutions uses these details to respond to your inquiry and discuss cleaning services with you.

The quote form is delivered to Country Roads by email through a secure form endpoint we operate (Google Apps Script). If campaign details are in the page address, they are included with your inquiry so we can see how you found us.

Please leave out sensitive information such as payment details, alarm codes, and door codes. Call or text (304) 710-5430 if we need to arrange a key or other access.
