Holiday cleaning checklist: what we cover

  • Kitchen: counters, surfaces, and the everyday buildup.
  • Bathrooms, especially the one everyone shares.
  • Guest rooms. Add a bed linen change with the laundry and linen add-on if you want one.
  • Living areas: dusting and floors, vacuumed and mopped.
  • A deep clean adds baseboards, ceiling fans, built-up kitchen grease and bathroom soap scum, and pet hair.
  • Extras, only if you ask: inside the oven and inside the fridge.
  • Not included: decorating, cooking, house-sitting, carpet shampooing, exterior windows, or pressure washing.
