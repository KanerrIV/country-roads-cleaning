Move-out cleaning

Leaving a rental or selling a home? We clean it top to bottom after the boxes are out so it is ready for the walkthrough, the landlord or the next owner. Serving Kenova, Ceredo, Huntington and nearby West Virginia, Kentucky and Ohio towns.

This is the clean for a walkthrough. We come after the boxes are out, not while a household is still packing around us. What is the difference between move-in and move-out cleaning?

Move-in cleaning is done before you unpack, including the kitchen, cabinets, bathrooms, floors, and corners the last occupants missed. Move-out cleaning is done after the boxes are out so the home is ready for a walkthrough, a landlord, or the next owner.
